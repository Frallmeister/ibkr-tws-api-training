# Defining U.S. stock contracts

> **Status:** Planned

## Purpose
Show clean, stock-only `Contract` construction using realistic U.S. equity examples.

## Intended coverage
- `secType="STK"`;
- `currency="USD"`;
- `exchange="SMART"`;
- `primaryExchange` where relevant;
- NASDAQ-focused examples without making NASDAQ an architectural assumption.

## Depends on
The `Contract` object.

## Leads into
Contract resolution and market-data requests.
